The AGPL permits copying, modifying, forking, and redistributing the software.
It does not grant permission to use the Gulden Marks in a way that suggests
official origin, sponsorship, or endorsement.

## Allowed Uses

Without separate permission, you may:

- use "Gulden" accurately to refer to the original project;
- link to the official repository or hosted service;
- state that an unmodified copy is powered by Gulden;
- use the project name in factual news, reviews, comparisons, and discussions;
  and
- describe a modified version as a fork or derivative of Gulden, provided the
  description is accurate and does not imply that it is official.

## Uses Requiring Permission or Changes

Do not, without written permission:

- use Gulden Marks as the name of a fork, hosted service, company, domain,
  application, or product;
- use Gulden Marks in advertising that implies endorsement or partnership;
- use the official logo or a confusingly similar logo for a modified version;
- register a domain, social account, package, or app-store listing that could
  be confused with the official Gulden project; or
- alter, recolor, or combine the official logo in a way that suggests official
  approval.

Forks and hosted services should use distinct names, logos, and domains. They
may include a factual notice such as "A Gulden fork" or "Powered by Gulden"
when that statement is accurate and not misleading.

copyright and license notices. Keeping the Gulden Marks in an unmodified copy
does not by itself grant permission to use them for a separate product or
service identity.
